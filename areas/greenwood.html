About Greenwood

Greenwood anchors the south side of the Indianapolis metro and has a longer development history than some of the newer Hamilton County suburbs, with established mid-century neighborhoods closer to Old Town Greenwood and newer subdivisions extending south and east. The US-31 corridor has brought significant commercial growth, and residential development has followed, giving Greenwood a mix of home ages and styles. Older sections of the city may need more roofing, gutter, and window work typical of homes several decades old, while newer neighborhoods lean toward fencing, landscaping, and routine lawn care. Johnson County's soil and drainage patterns can also make concrete leveling a fairly common need on driveways and walkways that have settled over time.

What Homeowners in Greenwood Ask

How does Greenwood's housing stock compare to Carmel or Fishers?

Greenwood has a somewhat longer development history, so it includes more established mid-century neighborhoods alongside newer subdivisions, whereas cities like Carmel and Fishers skew younger overall.

Is concrete leveling a common need in Greenwood?

It comes up fairly often. Settling driveways, walkways, and patios are a common issue in older Johnson County neighborhoods, and concrete leveling is usually far cheaper than full replacement.

Do I need a permit for a fence in Greenwood?

Greenwood generally requires permits for fences over a certain height and for most new decks. A local contractor can confirm current requirements for your property.

What's the best time of year to schedule exterior home projects in Greenwood?

Like the rest of central Indiana, Greenwood sees cold, freeze-prone winters and hot, humid summers, so most exterior work — painting, concrete leveling, deck building, roofing, and fencing — goes best in the late spring through early fall window when temperatures are consistently mild and rainfall is more predictable. Scheduling early in that window also tends to mean shorter wait times, since local contractors get busier as the weather warms and demand picks up across the metro.
